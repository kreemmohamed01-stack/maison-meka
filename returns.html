Returns & Exchanges

Every Maison Meka piece is checked by hand before it leaves us. To keep things simple and fair for everyone, our returns work on one clear rule — inspect your order at the door.

Returns are accepted only at the moment of delivery

Please open and inspect your order while the courier is still with you. If anything is not right, hand it back to the courier on the spot. Once the courier has left and the order has been accepted, it can no longer be returned or exchanged.

How returns work

Because many of our pieces are delicate, one-of-a-kind or sourced in very small numbers, we handle returns at the doorstep rather than after delivery. This protects you from receiving a piece that has been handled and re-shipped, and it lets us resolve any issue immediately.

  1. Wait for the courier to hand you the parcel — do not sign or pay before you have looked.
  2. Open the package in front of the courier and check the piece carefully: the item itself, its condition, its finish and any lid, base or accessory that comes with it.
  3. If everything is right, accept the order and enjoy your piece.
  4. If something is wrong — it is damaged, broken, incomplete or not the piece you ordered — refuse it and return it to the courier right away.
  5. Message us on Instagram with your name and order details so we can arrange a replacement or a solution with you.

When a return is accepted

Accepted

  • Returned to the courier during delivery
  • The piece arrived damaged or broken
  • The piece is not the one you ordered
  • Parts of the order are missing

Not accepted

  • Any request after the courier has left
  • A change of mind after accepting the order
  • Damage that happens after delivery
  • Natural variations of handmade or vintage pieces

Exchanges

Exchanges follow the same rule as returns: they are only possible if the piece is handed back to the courier at delivery. If you would like a different piece instead, let us know by message and we will do our best to arrange it, subject to availability.

Handmade, vintage and antique pieces

Many of our pieces are hand-painted, hand-blown or carry the marks of age. Small differences in colour, glaze, pattern or texture compared with photographs are part of their character and are not considered defects. Screen settings can also make colours look slightly different.

Refused orders

If an order is refused at the door without a valid reason, delivery charges may still apply, and we may ask for payment in advance on future orders.

In short: check it while the courier is there. That one minute at the door is what makes every return quick and easy.
