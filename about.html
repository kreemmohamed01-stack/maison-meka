Our Story

Maison Meka

A house of beautiful things, gathered with love and chosen to be lived with.

The House

Where every piece has a soul

Maison Meka began with a simple belief: a home should tell the story of the people who live in it. Not through more things, but through the right ones.

We search for pieces with character — Murano glass, fine porcelain, antiques, lamps and timeless decor — each one chosen by hand, inspected with care, and wrapped as if it were a gift for our own home.

Every object we offer is one we would proudly place in our own living room. That is our only rule.
