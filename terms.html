About us

Maison Meka is a curated home décor house offering decorative pieces, antiques, Murano glass, porcelain and objects for the home. Orders are confirmed and completed personally through Instagram.

Products and photos

We describe and photograph our pieces as accurately as possible. Handmade, hand-painted, vintage and antique items may show small variations, marks or signs of age that are part of their character. Colours may appear slightly different depending on your screen.

Prices

All prices are shown in Egyptian Pounds (EGP). Prices may change without notice, but the price confirmed with you when you place your order is the price you pay. Delivery charges are added separately and confirmed before your order is placed.

Orders and availability

Adding a piece to your bag does not reserve it. An order is only confirmed once we confirm it with you by message. Because many pieces are one of a kind, we may occasionally be unable to fulfil an order; if so, we will let you know as soon as possible.

Delivery

Delivery details, cost and estimated timing are agreed with you when your order is confirmed. Please see our Shipping & Delivery page for more.

Returns and exchanges

Returns and exchanges are accepted only at the moment of delivery, while the courier is present. Once an order has been accepted and the courier has left, it cannot be returned or exchanged. Full details are on our Returns & Exchanges page.
